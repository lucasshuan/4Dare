# Product

4Dare is a collection of games to play with friends. For now there is one: **Who am I?**, online, for 2 to 4 people.

## How to play

1. Someone creates a room (public or private) and shares the link or the code. Players mark themselves ready; the host starts the match when they like (if someone is not ready yet, the host is asked first).
2. A short opening plays: on the room's first match, a cold open that explains the game; on later ones, a "Round N" card.
3. Everyone has 20 s to vote on one of three themes, like "Superheroes", drawn from the theme sets the host turned on (movies, anime, sports...). Votes are open and can change; a tie goes to a wheel. Or the host just types the theme.
4. The chosen theme takes the stage. On the first match, the rule follows: "Every character must be from this theme", with two characters that fit (✓) and, when the theme allows a clear one, one that doesn't (✗).
5. The draw: every avatar hops into the 4 and a slip comes out with the player you pick for.
6. Each player fills a card for their target: type a name (the library suggests matches), tap one of the theme's popular characters in the hand at the bottom, or draw one at random. You can change the picture or create a new character. The card is saved as you go: when time runs out, it goes as it is, and an empty card gets a random character.
7. The cast: you see who picked your card, then the turn order. You see everyone's card but yours.
8. On your turn, you ask a yes-or-no question. Everyone answers: Yes, Probably, Don't know, Probably not, No or Doesn't matter, with a comment if they want.
9. Then you guess. The right name counts at once. If it's only close, whoever picked the character decides.
10. First to find out wins. At the end there's a podium; a few seconds later everyone is back in the lobby for another one.

Each step has a time limit, set per room: 80 s to ask and to answer, 60 s to guess, 40 s to check a guess (the theme vote always gets 20 s, picking 120 s). A step's clock starts when the scene before it ends. Every answer that leaves others still to answer cuts 20% of the answer time, so nobody waits long on the last one. Between steps, everyone sees the answers.

During turns, the history of questions, answers and guesses sits on the left (a drawer on phones), with filters.

## Chat

Every room has a chat, in the lobby and through the match: a panel at the bottom right on computers (new lines show as bubbles while it is folded), a bar at the bottom on phones. The game posts a line at the match's moments (match started, the theme, the turn order, whose turn it is). Lines are short (280 characters) and go when the room closes.

## Who plays

- Guest: no sign-up, gets a random name like BraveCat and a critter avatar.
- Discord or Google account: picks a name and avatar. Signing in mid-match keeps your seat and your chat lines.

## Extras

- English, Portuguese and Japanese.
- Character library with pictures. You can create a new one, change anyone's picture, or draw one people often picked for the theme.
- Light and dark. Works on phones. Reduced motion is respected: scenes keep their timing but drop the movement.
