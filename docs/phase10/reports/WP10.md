## What was built
- `CastScene({ show })` covers the received, order and entrance beats. It is one `useStageTimeline` that starts at `received.startsAt`, so a reload or a late join lands on the same frame.
  - **Table:** spec C 2.1 sizes (150/80 cards, gaps 26/8). The columns sit in turn order in the DOM. Each one starts shifted to its seat slot (measured with `layoutRect`), so the shuffle moves the same elements.
  - **Received, first match:**
    - Your "?" card falls from 520 px higher, at ×1.45 (phone ×1.65), with `bounceOut`. It pulses at 1.0 and moves to its slot at 2.4.
    - "[av] {name} picked yours." plays 0.1 to 2.3, then the rule line comes in at 2.55.
    - The others flip in seat order from 2.9 (+0.18 i).
  - **Received, later matches:** your card drops straight into its slot at 0.2. The others flip from 0.5 (+0.12 i). There is no rule line.
  - **Order:**
    - The kicker comes in.
    - The columns shuffle along an arc (`y ±46`, p2InOut on x) at 0.5 + 0.05 i (later: 0.2).
    - The badges come in at 1.6 + 0.16 i (later: 0.9 + 0.12 i).
    - At `orderSpot` the spotlight goes to `view.turn.playerId`: scale 1.1, a seat ring overlay at 90%, the others drop to .55. "[av] {name} starts!" lands at +0.05.
    - The table shrinks up (scale .32, y −330/−300, opacity 0, p3In) 0.5 s before the beat ends (later: 0.4 s), which is 4.4 / 2.0.
  - **Card lines:**
    - Under each card, a small "from {name}" / "by {name}" / "by you" line with the picker's avatar. It is shown on desktop only.
    - Under each column, a label with an avatar and the name ("you" for you). It truncates to card + gap, and the avatar is always kept.
  - **Away players** stay in the table at .55.
  - **Reduced motion** follows spec C 2.8: the same times, 0.2 s fades only.
- `TurnScreen`:
  - During received and order it renders only the scene, over a root that keeps its height.
  - From the entrance beat it renders the strip (`enter`) and the body (`Rise`: y 24→0, opacity, 0.6 s p3Out at +0.4) under the scene's exit. The scene is inert there (`aria-hidden`, `pointer-events-none`).
  - The entrance start is latched in state, so neither entrance is cut when the cast leaves the view.
  - Your own card's meta already reads "Picked by {name}" (WP1).
- `PlayerStrip({ players, enter? })`: the items drop in (y −30→0, opacity, 0.5 s `backOut(1.6)`, stagger 0.06) on the server clock. Without `enter` nothing changes.

## Deviations from the plan
1. **The scene stays through the entrance beat** as an absolute, inert overlay. The table's exit (4.4 + 0.7 = 5.1 s) runs past the end of the order beat (4.9). Unmounting it at 4.9 would cut the exit at about 25%.
2. **The strip and body mount at the entrance** instead of rendering hidden during the cast. A hidden Ask field would take the autofocus while it can't be seen.
3. **The entrance animates an inner `div`** in each strip item. The ring and background moved there from the `li`, so the timeline never writes the transform that the `li`'s `layout` animation owns.
4. **When a beat is shorter than its constant** (e2e `DARE_SHOW_SCALE`), the scene's times are multiplied by `beat / constant`. At scale 1 nothing changes.
5. **The exit is timed from the order beat's end and the spotlight from `CAST_MARKS.orderSpot`**, so trimming `order.*` trims holds, and the wash and the spotlight stay in sync.
6. **Headings use inline text instead of `flex-wrap`.** The avatar and name stay together (`inline-flex nowrap`; the name truncates only as a last resort), and the sentence wraps naturally in pt and ja. Phone sizes step down only for names over 10 characters (28→24, 40→30). `word-break: auto-phrase` gives clean ja breaks.
7. **All four headings share one grid cell**, so the row never moves between beats. The box gap is 56/40 in both beats (spec: 52 in order).
8. **The small lines split the translated text themselves**, not through `PlayerName`, so the name truncates while its avatar stays.
9. **No back face on the flip.** This follows the prototype (spec C notes it as optional polish).
10. **In later matches**, "picked yours" stays up until the order beat starts, and the kicker leaves at `orderSpot − 0.2`.
